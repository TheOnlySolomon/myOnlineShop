Our Story

Every journey begins with a single movement. At CloverCali, that journey grew into a pursuit of four disciplines — Muscle-Up, Planche, Front Lever, and Handstand. Each one represents a different battle.

The Muscle-Up represents power and progression, the Handstand represents balance and precision, the Front Lever represents body tension and mastery, and the Planche represents raw strength and control.

Together, they became what we call The Four Horsemen of Calisthenics.

Four movements that challenge every aspect of the athlete and demand more than strength alone. They demand patience, discipline, failure, and the willingness to return and try again.

Our journey isn't simply skills to collect; they are milestones on a path toward mastery. Your CloverCali story is your own journey, that gives you a unique goal. It is forged through every struggle, every failure, and every breakthrough.

Four Horsemen. One Undying Will. Master the Four. Earn Your Gravity.
